FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Quick answers to the most common questions
How do I set up my Minecraft server? ▼
After signing up, you'll get instant access to your Pterodactyl panel. From there, select your Minecraft version, click Deploy, and your server will be online within 30 seconds. We have full guides in our knowledge base and Discord.
Are the free plans really free forever? ▼
Yes! Our free plans have no time limit. Your server stays online as long as you keep it active (we pause servers inactive for 7+ days to free resources, but you can restart anytime). No credit card required.
What DDoS protection do you offer? ▼
All plans include DDoS protection. Free plans get standard layer 3/4 protection. Paid plans include advanced layer 7 application-level mitigation up to 1Tbps capacity, keeping your server online even during large attacks.
Can I install mods and plugins? ▼
Absolutely! The Pterodactyl panel supports one-click installation of Paper, Spigot, Forge, Fabric, NeoForge, and more. You can upload custom JARs, install mods, plugins, and resource packs directly from the panel.
How do I upgrade my plan? ▼
Log into your dashboard, click "Billing" in the sidebar, select your desired plan, and complete the payment. Upgrades are instant — your server gets the new resources immediately with no downtime.
What locations are available? ▼
We have data centers in: US-East (New York), US-West (Los Angeles), EU-West (Frankfurt), EU-North (Amsterdam), Asia-Pacific (Singapore), and Australia (Sydney). More locations are being added regularly.
